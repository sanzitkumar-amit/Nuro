# 🚀 How to Run NEURO (Super Easy Guide)

Here are the simplest instructions to run NEURO on your computer without any hassle or CMD windows!

---

## ⚡ Daily 1-Click Launch (The Easiest Way)

Every time you want to use NEURO:

1. Open your project folder: `d:\Code paradox\Code\Antigravity\Nuro`
2. **Double-click** on [`Start_NEURO_App.vbs`](file:///d:/Code%20paradox/Code/Antigravity/Nuro/Start_NEURO_App.vbs).
3. **Done!** 
   - The backend starts silently in the background.
   - The frontend starts silently.
   - Your browser opens `http://localhost:5173` automatically!

---

## 🖥️ Create a Desktop Shortcut (Optional - For Ultimate Ease)

To launch NEURO directly from your Windows Desktop:

1. Right-click on [`Start_NEURO_App.vbs`](file:///d:/Code%20paradox/Code/Antigravity/Nuro/Start_NEURO_App.vbs).
2. Click **Send to** ➡️ **Desktop (create shortcut)**.
3. Now you can double-click **Start_NEURO_App** right from your Desktop anytime!

---

## 🤖 Ensure AI Responses Work (Ollama Setup - 1 Time Only)

1. Make sure you installed Ollama from [ollama.com/download/windows](https://ollama.com/download/windows).
2. Look at the bottom-right corner of your screen (near the Windows clock). You should see the **Ollama icon** running.
3. If it's running, NEURO will automatically communicate with it!

---

## 🛑 How to Stop NEURO

When you are completely finished using NEURO and want to close background servers:
- Simply close your browser tab.
- If you ever need to stop all background Node/Python processes, open Task Manager and end `python.exe` and `node.exe` (or restart your computer).
